Donation methods

Cambridge Dev Lab, Inc. is a volunteer-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 42-2005486, Candid profile). Donations are tax-deductible and cover food for sessions, equipment like loaner laptops and running costs.

Zelle — send to classes@cambridge-dev.org. No processing fees. For a receipt, please email us after donating.

Employer matching — many employers match donations and volunteer hours. Look for Cambridge Dev Lab, Inc. (EIN 42-2005486) in Benevity or your company's giving portal.

Questions and receipts

Email classes@cambridge-dev.org. We provide donation acknowledgment letters for tax purposes.
